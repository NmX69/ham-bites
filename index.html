How a meeting works

Each module has two handouts, each with an audio guide. Bites are the conceptual material to present; Desserts are the rote material for home study.

  1. 1

    Print

    Hand out the module's two handouts at the meeting.

  2. 2

    Play

    Play the Bite's audio guide — about five minutes, never more than eight.

  3. 3

    Talk

    A short discussion afterwards lets those who've got it help those who don't. That's it.

Every module has an Exam Review

One recording covers the exam questions from both handouts. Each question is announced by its pool number and read out, then a pause to answer, then the correct answer and a brief reason. It works as a self-quiz, homework, or the closing activity of a meeting — and lets someone study the whole question bank by ear.

Go at your own pace

The modules are small, so they stack. Tech Bites, for example:

  • Standard — one module a week, about 12 weeks
  • Accelerated — two per session, about 6 sessions
  • Sprint — three per week, about 4 weeks

Every handout is audited line by line against the official NCVEC question pool — answer keys, teaching claims and figures — before it ships. When a pool is revised, the material is rebuilt and republished at the same addresses.
